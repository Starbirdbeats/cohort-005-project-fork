Round 3 · the deck, flat

Showing "more than one" without perspective

No cards shrinking into the distance. Every idea stays flat and never shows an exact count: with 10 unread you only see "there's more", but as you clear down to the last few you can watch what's left shrink. The "1 of 3" text is gone too. Each row runs 10 unread → 3 left → 2 left → 1 left.

Rule for every ideaShow at most a few "more" markers. More than that and it looks the same as 10. Under the cap, each × visibly removes one marker. On the last card, no marker at all.
Arrows stay, numbers go‹ › still flip. With one card left, the arrows disappear, which is itself the "last one" signal.

Round 4 · low-fi for approval

Segments deck + tabbed page, together

Segments (idea D) folded into the round 2 design. The compact view is a bell with a still dot. Clicking it opens one flat card with a thin segment bar on top: one segment per unread card, at most 5, with a dotted last segment when there are more. × or Open marks the card read and takes away one segment. With one card left there's no bar and no arrows. "All notifications" opens the tabbed page from iteration 1.

Segments don't move by themselvesThe bar never fills over time and cards never auto-advance. That's what keeps it from reading as a stories timer. The lit segment only moves when you flip.
New-event toastSame card, no segment bar, dropping out of the bell and hiding after about 6 s. × on it marks it read. It then joins the deck.
Still assumedNewest first. Open marks read. Flipping marks nothing read. Esc or clicking outside closes the deck. Cards closed with × show as read on the page's All tab.
